TUTOR SUBJECT OVERLAP ANALYSIS SUMMARY
=====================================

This analysis shows the top 5 matches for each tutor based on subject overlap.
The overlap score represents what percentage of a tutor's subjects are covered by another tutor.

OVERLAP SCORING METHOD:
- Overlap Score = (Number of subjects in common) / (Total subjects of the first tutor)
- Example: If Tutor A has 10 subjects and Tutor B covers 7 of them, the overlap is 70%

KEY FINDINGS:
- Total tutors analyzed: 67
- Subject overlap helps identify backup tutors and knowledge gaps
- Higher overlap scores indicate better backup coverage
- Lower overlap scores show complementary knowledge areas

REPORT FORMAT:
The full HTML report (tutor_overlap_report.html) includes:
1. Table of contents for easy navigation
2. Individual sections for each tutor
3. Top 5 matches with overlap percentages
4. Detailed breakdown of shared and unique subjects
5. Professional formatting suitable for sharing

USAGE:
- Open tutor_overlap_report.html in any web browser
- Use the table of contents to jump to specific tutors
- Green badges show overlap percentages
- Blue boxes show subject differences between tutors

This analysis helps identify:
- Which tutors can best cover for each other
- Knowledge gaps in the tutoring team
- Opportunities for cross-training
- Optimal tutor assignments for different subjects
